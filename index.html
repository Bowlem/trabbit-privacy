Inside the app

Built to track training the way it actually happens

Trabbit doesn't collapse everything into a single streak number. Each piece of your training gets tracked — and shown — the way it actually works.

Habits, honestly scheduled

Recurring habits that know your season — preseason, in-season, offseason, or rehab. A missed day shows as missed. A partial effort shows as partial. Your streak reflects what actually happened, right there on Today.

Built around your season

Setup starts by asking what phase you're actually in and what you're training for — not a generic one-size calendar. What's due today, and how backlog windows behave, adapts to the season you picked.

A journal for what doesn't fit in a checkbox

A place for the sessions that don't reduce to a tick — how a lift felt, what you noticed in a run, what to fix next time. Dictate it straight after a session, and lock it behind Face ID if you'd rather keep it private.

Every test, trending

Track the numbers that actually matter over a season — 40-yard splits, max lifts, mile times — and Trabbit tells you the moment you beat your own best, no interpretation required.

The full history, honestly

Every day you tracked, laid out at a glance — not just the last 7. A good stretch, or a bad one, is visible across weeks and months, not buried behind a single number.

Progress you can actually see

Plot any habit, test, or synced metric against any other and watch how they move together over a week, a month, or a season.

♥

Apple Health & WHOOP sync

Link a habit to steps, recovery, sleep, or strain and let it complete itself from real data — read-only, always. Your wearable does the logging.

◈

Real streaks, real milestones

Streaks are computed from what you actually completed — nothing fudged to keep a number alive. Milestones celebrate the ones that are real.
