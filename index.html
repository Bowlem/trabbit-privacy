The system

Four ways to track training, one honest record

Trabbit doesn't collapse everything into a single streak number. Each piece of your training gets tracked the way it actually works.

✓

Habits, honestly scheduled

Recurring habits that know your season — preseason, in-season, offseason, or rehab. A missed day shows as missed. A partial effort shows as partial. Your streak reflects what happened.

✎

Training logs

A journal for the sessions that don't reduce to a checkbox — how a lift felt, what you noticed in a run, what to fix next time. Written, not just logged.

◷

Performance tests

Track the numbers that actually matter over a season — 40-yard splits, max lifts, mile times — and watch the trend, not just the last result.

♥

Apple Health & WHOOP sync

Link a habit to steps, recovery, sleep, or strain and let it complete itself from real data — read-only, always. Your wearable does the logging.

◈

Real streaks, real milestones

Streaks are computed from what you actually completed — nothing fudged to keep a number alive. Milestones celebrate the ones that are real.

◔

Progress you can see

Plot any habit, test, or synced metric against any other and watch how they move together over a week, a month, or a season.
