Pro structure for every athlete.

Habits, notes and performance tests in one place, built around your season. Missed is missed, partial is partial. Trabit tracks what really happened, not what looks good.

See how it works

Four outcomes, not two.

Most trackers only know done or not done. Every habit in Trabit is recorded the way it really went.

On time
Done on the day it was due.
Late
Logged in the one-day catch-up window, and marked as late.
Partial
Some of the target, not all of it. Counted as exactly that.
Missed
Once the window closes, the day locks. No quiet rewrites.
Your training, three ways

One screen for today, the trend, and the record.

Your day, honestly scheduled

Habits grouped the way you train, due on the days you train. Tick them off, log an amount, or swipe one away as not done. Your streak and weekly consistency sit right on top, worked out from what actually happened.

  • Season-aware: preseason, in season, offseason or rehab
  • A ring for every day of the week
  • Milestones you can see coming

Know exactly where you stand

Completion over a week, a month, a season or all time, split into on time, late, partial and missed. Trabit points out what changed, and lets you plot any habit, test or synced metric against another.

  • Insights on what moved
  • Records, badges and a monthly report
  • Graph habits, logs and tests together

The full history, in the open

Every habit gets its own month calendar. Done days fill in, and habits with a target fill a ring toward it, so a strong stretch or a bad one is visible at a glance, not buried behind a streak.

  • One calendar per habit
  • Target habits fill toward the goal
  • Gaps have nowhere to hide
Beyond the checkbox

For the numbers and notes a habit can't hold.

Tests

Chase real PRs

40-yard splits, max lifts, jump heights, mile times. Training and official attempts side by side, and a trend line that tells you the moment you beat your best.

Notebook

A training journal

How a lift felt, what the coach said, what to fix next time. Write or dictate it straight after a session, tag it, lock it behind Face ID, and find it again in seconds.

Prompts

Reviews that stick

Scheduled prompts like a post-session review or a weekly reflection, with their own streak, so writing it down becomes part of the training.

Social
