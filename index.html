Inside the app

Built to track training the way it actually happens

Trabit doesn't collapse everything into one number. Every habit, note and test is tracked, and shown, the way it really went.

Today

Your day, honestly scheduled

Habits grouped the way you train, due on the days you train. Tick them off, log an amount, or swipe one away as not done. Forgot to log last night? You get one day to catch up, and it's marked as late, not quietly rewritten.

On time Late Partial Missed
Stats

Know exactly where you stand

Completion over a week, a month, a season or all time, split into on time, late, partial and missed. Trabit points out what changed, and lets you plot any habit, test or synced metric against another.

Calendar

The full history, in the open

Every habit gets its own month calendar. Done days fill in, and habits with a target fill a ring toward it, so a strong stretch or a bad one is visible at a glance, not buried behind a streak.

Notebook

A notebook for what a checkbox can't hold

Scheduled prompts like a post-session review, plus free notes whenever you need them. How a lift felt, what the coach said, what to fix next time.

  • Dictate it straight after a session
  • Lock it behind Face ID
  • Search every past note
Tests

Every test, trending

40-yard splits, max lifts, jump heights, mile times. Log training and official attempts separately, watch the trend line, and Trabit tells you the moment you beat your best.

Social

A rating you can't fake

Your Consistency Rating is how much of your own plan you actually hit, worked out on our servers from what you logged. Climb the global board or just your friends'. Everyone sees your name and rating, and nothing else. Your habits stay private.

Widgets

Your streak on the Home Screen

See today's progress and your streak without opening the app, or jump straight into today's note. Widgets follow the app's light or dark look.

♥

Apple Health & WHOOP

Link a habit to steps, exercise minutes, recovery, sleep or strain and it completes itself from real data. Read-only, always. Your wearable does the logging.

Built around your season

Preseason, in season, offseason or rehab. Habits only come due in the phases you set, so a deload never reads as a slump.

✦

Set up in a minute

Pick starter habits made for your sport, scheduled on the days you said you'll train. Change any of it later.

◷

Reminders that know what's left

A nudge at the time you pick for each habit, an evening check on what's still open, and a warning before yesterday's catch-up window closes.
